## Use of Public Writeups

Public writeup indexes were reviewed only to understand broad presentation patterns such as separating recon, evidence, initial access, lateral movement, privilege boundaries, and reporting. No individual solution, active challenge, Sherlock package, flag, credential, target-specific exploit chain, or proprietary content was copied or compiled into this repository.

The skills must not be used to scrape or transform HTB content into a dataset, knowledge base, training corpus, evaluation set, benchmark, or synthetic derivative. Users who want to publish a writeup must verify that the target is allowed for publication under the current HTB Platform Rules.

## User-Supplied Material

`nimux-command-surface.txt` is included because it was supplied directly by the user. It is treated as version-sensitive local documentation. The local `nimux --help` output, local documentation, explicit authorization, and the lab readme take precedence over it.
